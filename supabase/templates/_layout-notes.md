Shared shape of these three emails, so a change to one is a deliberate
divergence rather than a drift:

- Tables and inline styles only. Gmail strips <style> blocks, Outlook ignores
  flexbox, and a broken confirmation email costs an account.
- 600px maximum width, centred, on #f1f5f9.
- Brand blue #0068f8 for the single action button; ink #0f172a for headings.
- The raw link is printed under the button. Some mail clients on cheap Android
  handsets do not make buttons tappable, and the URL is the fallback.
- One action per email. Nothing to click except the thing it is for.
